## In priority order. Ship each one as you finish it, do not batch.

### 1. TIN (0-35 min) - highest impact, it is on every roof in the village
Her verdict: "tin is not looking like tin." Currently perfectly regular clean ribs,
reading as a painted barcode.
- **Vary the rib pitch and depth** across the sheet. Perfect repetition is the tell.
- **Dents and buckles**: push a few ribs out of line, especially near edges.
- **Rust blooming from the bottom edge upward** and around fixing points, not a flat
  rust tint over the whole sheet.
- **Fixing washers** at the laps, and a visible overlap line where sheets meet with a
  shadow under it.
- **Sheen varies along the ribs**: the crowns catch light, the valleys stay dull.
  A uniform roughness over corrugation is what makes it read as plastic.

### 2. TERRACOTTA (35-65 min) - the other roof material
Her verdict: plastic wrap. Currently one brown smear with no tile shapes at all.
- **Model the tiles as real overlapping geometry** (Mangalore pattern). This cannot be
  painted on a flat face.
- Slight per-tile height and rotation jitter so the rows are not machine-straight.
- Moss or dirt in the channels between tiles.
- A few cracked tiles and a few paler replacement ones.

### 3. PAINTED WOOD (65-90 min) - doors, shutters, posts, carts
Her verdict: plastic wrap. Currently a green-grey smear.
- **Real plank separation as geometry**: gaps between planks with a shadow line.
- Grain running ALONG the plank, not across.
- Paint worn through to bare wood at the edges and around hinges and handles.
- Per-plank colour variation so a door is not one flat green.
