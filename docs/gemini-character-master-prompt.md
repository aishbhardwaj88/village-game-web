Two images are attached. The FIRST is the character design to reproduce. The SECOND is a finished
character from the same game, attached only as the benchmark for skin tone, material quality and
render style.

YOUR TASK: a material re-render of the first character. Not a redesign, not a new character.

KEEP EXACTLY from the first image: the face and every facial feature, eye shape and size, eyebrows,
head size, body proportions, height, build and weight, age, hairstyle, every garment with its cut,
length and colour, every accessory, and the overall stylised character-design language. If unsure
about anything, keep the original.

SKIN TONE: match the benchmark image exactly, sampled from its face, arms and hands. A light-to-medium
warm golden brown with a soft peachy undertone. Not dark, not grey, not ashy, not olive, not tanned,
not pale. Consistent across face, neck, arms, hands, legs and feet, only slightly deeper on forearms
and shins from sun. Everyone in this game is from one village and their tones must sit in one family.

EXPRESSION: warm and alive. A soft closed-mouth smile with the mouth corners clearly lifted, cheeks
slightly raised, bright engaged eyes, relaxed level eyebrows, chin level, looking straight ahead.
Never blank, never sad, never grinning.

MATERIALS: in the first image everything reads as smooth shiny injection-moulded plastic, like a toy.
Re-render with real physical surfaces:
Skin: fully matte, zero specular highlight, fine skin grain, soft warm subsurface at the ears, nose
and fingertips. No shine on forehead, cheeks, knuckles, forearms or shins.
Hair: many individual strands with visible separation and direction, a few soft flyaways, real depth
and shadow between layers. Braids show clear strand direction. Never a moulded shape or glossy shell.
Ribbons, bows, hair ties: real satin or cotton ribbon with a fine woven surface and a soft directional
sheen, never a hard highlight. Loops slightly uneven, visible creases and pinching at the knot, tails
hanging with real weight and slightly frayed ends.
Cotton (shirts, kurtas, uniforms, dhotis, petticoats): visible woven thread texture, slightly fuzzy
edges, natural creases and gathers at waist, elbows, knees and wherever fabric folds or stretches.
Matte, washed soft, clean and intact.
Chiffon, georgette, silk (sari, dupatta): fine sheer weave, soft translucency at the edges, pleats
falling with real weight, low soft sheen only.
Knit (socks, sweaters): visible ribbing and fibre fuzz.
Leather (shoes, chappals, belts, straps): grain, creases where it flexes, scuffs at toe and heel,
compressed worn sole. Matte to low sheen.
Canvas, nylon, jute (bags, sacks): coarse woven texture, visible stitching, slightly worn edges.
Thread and woven bands: twisted cotton fibre with visible individual strands.
Metal (buckles, clips, watches, bangles, brass, steel): the only material allowed to be reflective,
slightly worn with fine scratches, never mirror-perfect.
Glass bangles: true glass, transparent with colour, small internal highlights.
Plastic items: dull and used, never glossy.
Rule: roughness high on everything except metal and glass. Nothing wet, waxed, polished or moulded.

HANDS: both hands empty and open, fingers relaxed and slightly apart. Remove any object the character
is holding in the reference, including pots, ladles, trays, tools, books, slates, bags, food or money.
Those are modelled separately. A backpack worn on the shoulders may stay.

POSE: exactly one figure. Full body from head to feet, straight-on front view. A true A-pose: both
arms held symmetrically away from the body at about thirty degrees, with a clear visible gap between
each arm and the torso, elbows straight, palms facing inward, shoulders level, feet slightly apart,
weight even.

IMAGE FORMAT: a tall portrait image, taller than it is wide, roughly 3:4 or 9:16. The figure fills
almost the entire height of the frame with only a small margin above the head and below the feet.
No wide empty grey areas to the left and right. Maximum resolution and maximum surface detail, sharp
enough to see the weave in the cloth, individual strands in the hair and grain in the leather.

BACKGROUND AND LIGHT: plain flat mid-grey background, soft even studio lighting from the front, no
rim light, no dramatic shadows, no cast shadow on the background, no ground plane.

OUTPUT: one single final image, no variations, no side-by-side comparisons, no text, no logos,
no watermarks.
