## 1. A road that connects to nothing
Her words: "What is this? Some random road which is not connecting anywhere. At least
connect this road to somewhere."
Screenshot: a brown dirt strip with timber kerbs either side, running between the white
plastered buildings, that simply ENDS in open grass. Its end is a ragged edge in the
middle of a field, with nothing at the end and no junction.
Also visible in the same shot, not yet her complaint: a second identical strip to the
left, also ending in grass.

**Fix:** no road, lane or track may end in open ground. Every one either joins another
road, or ends at something that explains it (a field gate, a well, a shrine, a
compound gate). This is the same rule she gave for the map on 28 Oct: "end each road in
something". Audit EVERY road and lane for dead ends, not just this one.

## 2. Bazaar shops read as half shut. Raise the shutters and the awning.

Maybe the tin can be more up, and this way shutter can be more up so that front of the

Applies to EVERY shop in the bazaar row and to any other shuttered shop in the village
(general store, chemist, medicine, tailor, mobile, bangle, ration), not just the two in
the shot.

## 3. The roads themselves are badly made. Hard, structural fault.
Her words: "You really need to take care of the road. Roads are half cut, not clean, and
have very sharp edges. Roads don't have such sharp edges. They are usually curved and
round edges."
Screenshot: a junction seen from above. Every road is a flat brown rectangle laid on the
grass. The faults visible in this one shot:
- **Sharp straight-cut edges.** Each strip ends in a hard angular chevron or a clean
  diagonal slice, like a cut sheet of paper lying on a lawn.
- **Half cut.** Strips stop mid-air and start again, leaving notches and bites out of the
  surface. The road surface is not continuous.
- **No junctions.** Where strips cross, they overlap as separate rectangles instead of
  merging into one piece of road.
- **No edge at all.** The road meets the grass on a hard line, with nothing between.
- Timber kerb rails float beside the strips, not following the road edge.

**Fix. This is not a tweak, the roads need rebuilding as roads:**
1. **Round every edge.** A village road's edge is worn, irregular and soft, never a
   straight cut. Vary the edge line along its length.
2. **One continuous surface per road.** No notches, no gaps, no overlapping rectangles.
   Where two roads meet, build a real junction that merges into one surface.
3. **Give the edge a transition.** A road does not meet grass on a line: there is scuffed
   earth, loose gravel and thinning grass for a short band either side.
4. **Kerbs follow the road edge**, including round the curve, or they are left out.
5. Judge it from the player camera at standing height, not from above.

**Related to item 1 (road ending in nothing): treat the whole road network as one job.**

## 5. TWO petrol pumps and TWO dharam kantas in the world. BUG, fix first.
Her words: "I am very, very confused. There are two dharmkata, there are two petrol
pumps. What is happening? Have you not removed old places when you build the new one?"

**Cause, verified in code:**
- `createHighwayCluster` in `src/farmSites.js` builds a petrol pump and a dharam kanta
  cabin into its own geometry, at the old `highway` zone position.
- `src/main.js` then registers two EXACT asset slots pinning `petrol_pump.glb` and
  `dharam_kanta.glb` to that same old highway position (lines ~983 and ~988).
- The v2 versions (`yard_petrol`, `yard_dharam_kanta`) were separately placed at their
  1 Nov map spots.
- So each exists twice. The old post office, ration shop and chakki WERE switched off the
  same way when their v2 yards went in. These two were missed.

**Fix:**
1. Stop the highway cluster drawing its own pump and weighbridge geometry.
2. Delete the two exact slot registrations in `src/main.js`.
3. Keep only the map-placed v2 versions.
4. Then re-run the duplicate audit below and put it in the build checks so this cannot
   happen again:
   for every `X_v2.glb` that also has an `X.glb`, fail the build if the old one is still
   referenced in `src/`.

**Audit result at the time of writing: ONLY these two are duplicated. Nothing else.**
