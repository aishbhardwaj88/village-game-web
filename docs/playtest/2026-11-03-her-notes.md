# Her playthrough notes, 3 Nov. APPEND ONLY. She is playing and listing faults.

She is mid-playthrough and does not want replies. Record every item verbatim. Do not
start fixing until she says she is done, so the list is not worked out of order.

## 1. A road that connects to nothing
Her words: "What is this? Some random road which is not connecting anywhere. At least
connect this road to somewhere."
Screenshot: a brown dirt strip with timber kerbs either side, running between the white
plastered buildings, that simply ENDS in open grass. Its end is a ragged edge in the
middle of a field, with nothing at the end and no junction.
Also visible in the same shot, not yet her complaint: a second identical strip to the
left, also ending in grass.

**Fix:** no road, lane or track may end in open ground. Every one either joins another
road, or ends at something that explains it (a field gate, a well, a shrine, a
compound gate). This is the same rule she gave for the map on 28 Oct: "end each road in
something". Audit EVERY road and lane for dead ends, not just this one.

## 2. Bazaar shops read as half shut. Raise the shutters and the awning.
Her words: "It's looking like all the shops are half closed. Shutter needs to be more up.
Maybe the tin can be more up, and this way shutter can be more up so that front of the
shop is completely visible."
Screenshot: the bazaar row (tailor shop, medicine shop). The rolled shutter hangs down
over the top third of each opening and the tin awning sits low above it, so the opening
reads as a letterbox slit. The counter, the sewing machine and the shelves inside are
cut off at the top.

**Fix, in her own order:**
1. Raise the tin awning first, so there is room above the opening.
2. Then roll the shutters further up, so only a shallow rolled bundle shows at the head
   of each opening.
3. The whole shop front, counter to shelf top, must be visible from standing eye level
   on the street. Check it from the player camera, not from a side render.
Applies to EVERY shop in the bazaar row and to any other shuttered shop in the village
(general store, chemist, medicine, tailor, mobile, bangle, ration), not just the two in
the shot.
