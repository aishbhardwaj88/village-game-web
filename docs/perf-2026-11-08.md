# Perf profile, 8 Nov 2026 (P1)

Method: src/perfProbe.js sections in the frame loop, driven by tmp/perf-profile.mjs (headless Chrome, SwiftShader software GL, so absolute fps is 0.3-2 and NOT her Mac; only the split and the before/after ratios mean anything).

## Top costs found (headless, ms per frame, before)
1. standOn settle passes (feet-on-ground): single passes of 0.3-14 s each, five stages per entry, 40+ entries. Spawn 2279, chauraha 3832, bazaar 35586 ms/frame averaged.
2. bazaarLife.update: 235-312 ms/frame (re-seat of one walker per frame, 10 ray probes each, and a whole-scene index rebuild every second from a per-frame caller).
3. camRig 16-26 ms/frame (not yet touched). Collision 0.0 ms/frame (not a cost); render is GPU-bound and unmeasurable in software GL.

## Changes made
- standOn: settle probe bucket 8 cm -> 20 cm, stages 5 -> 3 (0 / 3 s / 40 s), settleInstances and bazaar_life time-sliced to 3 ms a frame, moving animals re-seat at most every 400 ms, scene index rebuild at most every 5 s and never from a per-frame caller.
- bazaarLife: walker re-seat every 6th frame, centre point only, cached index.

## After (same method)
bazaarLife 235-312 -> 4-9 ms/frame. Settle passes now run as slices of at most ~3 ms plus an occasional index rebuild; no single pass runs seconds. standOn.tick still averages high in headless because the main thread is starved by software GL.

## Feet check
tools/feet-check.js: 218 figures, 45 off by more than 3 cm, IDENTICAL to HEAD before these changes (arm_frontage people 12 cm sunk on arm_frontage_metal, gully_life people 20 cm into gully_life_concrete). Pre-existing, not caused by this change.

## Draw calls by group (tools/calls-by-group.mjs, headless, preview build, 8 Nov)
Chauraha view: 150 calls, 1.00M browser triangles (counter includes the shadow pass). Spawn view: 80 calls, 164k triangles.
Visible meshes by top-level group at the chauraha (scene-graph count, not frustum-filtered): village_kit_houses 65, place_batches 38, village_static_merged 30, life_scenes_2 15, trees 13, chauraha_edges 10, then many 7-10 call places and ride nooks.
Finding: the post-6-Nov scenes are NOT the batching problem. arm_frontage is 5 calls, gully_life 9, main_road_life 11, chauraha_edges 10, cyclist_events 2, each already merged per material. They also build lazily after frame 40, so adding them to the main mergeAcrossGroups list would do nothing.
The big remaining groups are village_kit_houses (already one merged mesh per material per area) and place_batches; cutting those further means merging across areas, which trades against the 350 m draw range and needs a look at pop-in.

## Items checked and closed without a code change
- Item 5 (shadows): the sun rig only follows the player behind ?shadows=follow (off), so the shadow box sits at the origin, 600 m from the village. The shadow pass draws nothing there. A range cap would change nothing until follow shadows are switched on.
- Item 3 (clutter LOD): ride nooks are already zone-streamed (build 60 m, visible 75 m, teardown 80 m). Merged static clusters of radius <= 60 m already carry a distGate, but at 350 m, because merging mixes buildings with props; an 80 m gate for props would need props split out of the shared merge. Not done.
- Item 4 (collision broad-phase): staticBoxesNear is already grid-based, collision measured 0.0 ms/frame.
