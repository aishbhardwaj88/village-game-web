# Perf profile, 8 Nov 2026 (P1)

Method: src/perfProbe.js sections in the frame loop, driven by tmp/perf-profile.mjs (headless Chrome, SwiftShader software GL, so absolute fps is 0.3-2 and NOT her Mac; only the split and the before/after ratios mean anything).

## Top costs found (headless, ms per frame, before)
1. standOn settle passes (feet-on-ground): single passes of 0.3-14 s each, five stages per entry, 40+ entries. Spawn 2279, chauraha 3832, bazaar 35586 ms/frame averaged.
2. bazaarLife.update: 235-312 ms/frame (re-seat of one walker per frame, 10 ray probes each, and a whole-scene index rebuild every second from a per-frame caller).
3. camRig 16-26 ms/frame (not yet touched). Collision 0.0 ms/frame (not a cost); render is GPU-bound and unmeasurable in software GL.

## Changes made
- standOn: settle probe bucket 8 cm -> 20 cm, stages 5 -> 3 (0 / 3 s / 40 s), settleInstances and bazaar_life time-sliced to 3 ms a frame, moving animals re-seat at most every 400 ms, scene index rebuild at most every 5 s and never from a per-frame caller.
- bazaarLife: walker re-seat every 6th frame, centre point only, cached index.

## After (same method)
bazaarLife 235-312 -> 4-9 ms/frame. Settle passes now run as slices of at most ~3 ms plus an occasional index rebuild; no single pass runs seconds. standOn.tick still averages high in headless because the main thread is starved by software GL.

## Feet check
tools/feet-check.js: 218 figures, 45 off by more than 3 cm, IDENTICAL to HEAD before these changes (arm_frontage people 12 cm sunk on arm_frontage_metal, gully_life people 20 cm into gully_life_concrete). Pre-existing, not caused by this change.
